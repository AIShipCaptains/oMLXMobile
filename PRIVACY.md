# Privacy Policy

**oMLX Mobile** does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

- **No analytics, tracking, or telemetry.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Chat history** is stored locally on your device (SwiftData) and never leaves it except when you send a message to a server you configured.
- **API keys** are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device and are sent only to the server address you configure.
- **Network traffic** goes exclusively to the server endpoints you explicitly add (for example, your own oMLX server). The developer operates no servers and receives no data.
- **On-device files**: conversations and settings live in the app sandbox and are removed when you delete the app.
